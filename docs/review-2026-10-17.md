## Scope

The review covered typed parameters and canonical artifacts, all implemented
generators, objective metrics, observer and synthetic-analysis contracts,
media adapters, serialized manifests, CLI commands, evidence and scholarship,
publication/release validation, packaging, CI, and documentation. Independent
read-only reviews supplied real-array and real-file reproductions before fixes.
The baseline was 171 passing tests and 90.21% branch-inclusive coverage.

## Reproducibility and compatibility

Corrections to stimulus construction intentionally change affected canonical
digests. Old manifests remain records of old requests/artifacts, not evidence
that corrected and previous stimuli are equivalent. Regenerate publication
outputs and media sidecars from the corrected sources. Version `0.5.0` and its
DOIs identify the archived release; this development pass does not mint a new
release or rewrite that archive.

Runtime resources under `src/duckrabbit/_resources/` mirror the canonical
evidence matrix and bibliography. Update both copies together; tests enforce
byte equality. Installed wheels support ordinary generation, evidence loading,
and claim APIs without a checkout. Manuscript rendering and checkout/release
audits still require the source tree and its assets.

CI covers Python 3.10, 3.12, 3.14, and 3.15, lint, the 90% coverage gate, offline
scholarship validation, publication regeneration, strict release auditing,
and a real installed-wheel smoke test outside the checkout.

## Evidence boundaries and remaining work

- Tests establish construction, numerical, serialization, and delivery
  invariants. They do not establish that a participant experiences an illusion.
- Synthetic diagnostics retain `human_data=false`; simulated observer records
  are not empirical psychophysics or estimates of human sensitivity.
- McGurk remains `input_required` until lawful checksummed speech fixtures,
  presentation contracts, and an observer-validation protocol exist.
- Offline evidence validation does not establish live DOI resolution. No live
  scholarship status is newly asserted by this review.
- Figure bytes depend on the selected platform font. Compare deterministic
  regenerations within the same environment, or pin `DUCKRABBIT_FONT_PATH`.
- The sibling template renderer and full manuscript/PDF production are
  separate integration surfaces, not certified by package-only checks.
